Already cataloging somewhere else?

If your collection is in PastPerfect or another program, it may be possible to bring it into Virtual Archive — it depends on what your export has to work with, but if the fields are there, it usually can be done. This isn't something you download and run yourself; it's a service we do by request. Tell us a bit about your collection (what program it's in, roughly how many items) and we'll let you know.
