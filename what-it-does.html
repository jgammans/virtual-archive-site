What it does

Everything Virtual Archive can do, in one place.

Smart Search

Find any item by donor, location, category, description, or anything typed about it — instantly.

Albums & Slideshows

Group items into albums, then click one picture and click another to put it there — no dragging required. Step through the result full-screen, or turn it into a book.

OCR Text Detection

Every picture you attach gets checked in the background for readable text. Find one, and an OCR button appears on the item so you can read it — a scanned letter never just sits there looking like a photo.

Accession & Donor Records

Museum mode tracks accession numbers, donor details, and produces a deed of gift ready to print and sign.

Backup & Restore

Your whole collection backs up to one file, and restores from it just as easily if you ever need to.

Windows, macOS & Linux

The same program and the same collection format everywhere — move between machines freely.

Your Own Report Templates

Design a card or report layout yourself in HTML or RTF, using plain labels like #TITLE#, #THUMB# and #DESCRIPTION# — the program fills each one in automatically when it builds the page. A picture label can even go by two names (#THUMB# or #FILENAME#) if that's what feels natural in your layout.

Publish a Category Online

Turn a whole category — up to 400 items, in whatever order you've set — into one HTML page with your own heading and write-up, ready to publish on a website or print to PDF.

Fields That Fit Your Collection

Thirteen extra fields you can rename to suit what you're cataloging. A rock collection can ask for Species, Locality and Hardness, while a museum collection right beside it still asks for Contributor(s) and Appraised Value.

More Than One Collection

One copy of the program can hold several collections, each entirely separate — a historical society and a personal mineral collection can sit side by side without ever mixing.

GPS & Photo Metadata

A photo's location and camera details, when it has them, show right alongside the item — useful for anything you're cataloging straight from a phone or camera.

Music Import

Point it at a folder of MP3s and it reads the tags for you — filed under Music/Artist/Album, named by track number and title, with the embedded album art as the picture.
